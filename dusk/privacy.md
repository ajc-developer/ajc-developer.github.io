# Artemis Solunar: Hunt & Fish Privacy Policy

_Effective October 27, 2026_

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

Artemis Solunar: Hunt & Fish ("the app") is made by ajc-dev ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

## The short version

* Artemis Solunar calculates sunrise, sunset, moon, and feeding times on your phone. It has no accounts, and we run no servers that receive your data.
* Your location and saved locations stay on your device. We never receive them.
* Widget settings (chosen saved location or follow-app selection) are stored on your device only.
* The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.
* Solunar Pro is a one-time purchase that removes all ads, unlocks Red light mode, and adds home-screen widgets. Sunrise, sunset, moon and feeding times are always free in the app. Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card details.

## Location

If you allow it, Artemis Solunar uses your phone's **approximate** location only to calculate times for where you are. You can also enter a location by hand instead. Your location and saved locations are stored only on your device, and Artemis Solunar never sends them to us. Widget settings (chosen saved location or follow-app selection) are stored on your device only. You can turn off location access at any time in your phone's settings.

## Offline use and network access

All of Artemis Solunar's features work without an internet connection. The app uses the network only to load ads in the free version and to check your Solunar Pro purchase with Google Play.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are muted. To show, limit, and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use:

* your device's advertising ID
* your IP address and the approximate location that can be worked out from it
* basic device and app information, and how you interact with ads

This data goes directly to Google under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google explains how it uses data from apps that show its ads [here](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Where the law requires it, including in the EEA and the UK, Artemis Solunar asks for ad consent (Google's consent form) before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice in Settings under Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

## Purchases

Solunar Pro is a one-time purchase that removes all ads, unlocks Red light mode, and adds home-screen widgets. Sunrise, sunset, moon and feeding times are always free in the app. The purchase is processed by Google Play under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Artemis Solunar receives only confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can remove all ads, unlock Red light mode, add home-screen widgets, and restore the purchase on a new phone. We never receive your payment details.

## What we don't do

We don't create accounts, ask for your name or email, run our own analytics, or sell your personal information.

## Children

Artemis Solunar is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold your data, deleting the app removes everything Artemis Solunar stored on your phone. For data collected by Google for ads, you can use your phone's ad settings or [Google's controls](https://myadcenter.google.com/). Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights, such as opting out of targeted advertising. Contact us with any request and we'll help where we can.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date.
